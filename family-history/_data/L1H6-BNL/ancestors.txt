Brief Life History of Abijah

Abijah Ballew was born in 1848, in Tennessee, United States. He had at least 7 daughters with Anna Liza Buckles. He lived in Liberty, Clay, Missouri, United States in 1890 and Liberty Township, Clay, Missouri, United States in 1900. He died in 1930, in Kansas City, Wyandotte, Kansas, United States, at the age of 82.

Name Meaning
Ballew
Abijah

Variant of Ballow 1, a surname of probable French origin (see Ballou 1 and 2). Compare Bellew 2.

English: variant of Ballow 2.

Possibly also an altered form of English, Irish, and Scottish Bellew 1.
