Brief Life History of Thomas Samuel

When Thomas Samuel Wade was born on 9 November 1899, in Covington, Kenton, Kentucky, United States, his father, Orin Lorinza Wade, was 30 and his mother, Caroline Losey, was 29. He married Frances Della Thompson on 19 July 1921, in Jackson, Ohio, United States. They were the parents of at least 1 son and 2 daughters. He lived in United States in 1949 and Coventry Township, Summit, Ohio, United States in 1950. He died on 30 October 1993, in Florida, United States, at the age of 93.

Name Meaning
Wade
Thomas
Samuel

English: from the Middle English personal name Wade (Old English Wada or, via Norman French, from ancient Germanic Wado, of unclear origin but possibly related to Old English wadan ‘to go’).

English: topographic name from Middle English wade ‘ford’ (Old English (ge)wæd ‘ford, especially a tidal crossing’) for someone who lived by a ford, or a habitational name from a place so called, such as Wade Hall in North Cove (Suffolk).

MORE

Irish: shortened and altered from Mac Uaid, see McQuaid .
