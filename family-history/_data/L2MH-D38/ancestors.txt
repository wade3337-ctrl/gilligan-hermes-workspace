Brief Life History of John Ray

When John Ray Hanley was born on 7 December 1888, in Missouri, United States, his father, Albridge T. Hanley, was 28 and his mother, Anna Malida Metcalf, was 28. He married Bertha Ballew on 3 June 1914, in Kansas City, Wyandotte, Kansas, United States. They were the parents of at least 2 sons and 3 daughters. He lived in Kansas City, Jackson, Missouri, United States for about 15 years and El Monte Judicial Township, Los Angeles, California, United States in 1940. He died on 7 December 1947, in Los Angeles, California, United States, at the age of 59, and was buried in Rose Hills Memorial Park, Whittier, Los Angeles, California, United States.

Name Meaning
Hanley
John
Ray

Some characteristic forenames: Irish Brendan, Conor, Declan, Donovan, Eamonn, John Patrick.

Irish: shortened form of O'Hanley, an Anglicized form of Gaelic Ó hÁinle ‘descendant of Áinle’, a personal name meaning ‘champion’. This is the name of a former ruling family in Connacht and is now common throughout southern Ireland.

MORE

English: habitational name from any of the many places called Handley (Cheshire, Derbyshire, Dorset, Northamptonshire) or Hanley (Staffordshire, Worcestershire), named with Old English hēan, the weak dative form (originally used after a preposition and article) of hēah ‘high’ + lēah ‘woodland clearing’.
