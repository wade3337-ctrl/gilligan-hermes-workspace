RHODA MAY HANLEY, daughter of John Ray HANLEY and Bertha BALLEW.
She married SCHUESSLER SAMUEL (John) GAUNT, 20 November 1932 in Webb City, Jasper County, Missouri. Schuessler Samuel (John) GAUNT, son of Harry Clinton GAUNT and Dorothy (Dorsie) Dell SCHUESSLER. She married (2. Glen Curtis HEEB.
