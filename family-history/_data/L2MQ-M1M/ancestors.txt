Brief Life History of Dorcie Dell

When Dorcie Dell Gaunt was born on 15 January 1935, in Missouri, United States, her father, Schessuler Samuel John Gaunt, was 23 and her mother, Rhoda May Hanley, was 19. She married Donald Lee Ainsworth on 12 May 1954, in Yuma, Arizona, United States. She died on 13 November 1997, in El Monte, Contra Costa, California, United States, at the age of 62, and was buried in Rose Hills Memorial Park, Whittier, Los Angeles, California, United States.
