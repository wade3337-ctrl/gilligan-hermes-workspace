Brief Life History of Bertha

When Bertha Ballew was born on 3 July 1895, in Missouri, United States, her father, Abijah Ballew, was 47 and her mother, Anna Liza Buckles, was 46. She married John Ray Hanley on 3 June 1914, in Kansas City, Wyandotte, Kansas, United States. They were the parents of at least 2 sons and 3 daughters. She lived in Kansas City, Jackson, Missouri, United States for about 15 years and El Monte Judicial Township, Los Angeles, California, United States in 1940. She died on 19 September 1950, in Los Angeles, California, United States, at the age of 55.
