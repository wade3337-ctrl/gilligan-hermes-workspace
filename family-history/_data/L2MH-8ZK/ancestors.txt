Brief Life History of Schessuler Samuel John

When Schessuler Samuel John Gaunt was born on 7 October 1911, in Lone Elm, Anderson, Kansas, United States, his father, Harry Clinton Gaunt, was 25 and his mother, Dorothy D Schuessler, was 21. He had at least 2 sons and 1 daughter with Rhoda May Hanley. He lived in Kansas City, Jackson, Missouri, United States in 1930. He died on 30 September 1960, in Baldwin Park, Los Angeles, California, United States, at the age of 48, and was buried in Whittier, Los Angeles, California, United States.
