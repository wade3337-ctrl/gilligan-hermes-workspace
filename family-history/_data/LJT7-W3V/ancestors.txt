Brief Life History of Orin Lorinza

When Orin Lorinza Wade was born on 27 October 1869, in Lawrence, Ohio, United States, his father, Samuel G. Wade, was 39 and his mother, Emily A. Kyle, was 39. He married Caroline Losey on 22 September 1896, in Hamilton, Ohio, United States. They were the parents of at least 3 sons and 4 daughters. He lived in Kenton, Kentucky, United States in 1900 and Wellston, Jackson, Ohio, United States for about 10 years. He died on 1 September 1953, at the age of 83, and was buried in Hebbardsville, Athens, Ohio, United States.

Name Meaning
Wade
Orin
Lorinza

English: from the Middle English personal name Wade (Old English Wada or, via Norman French, from ancient Germanic Wado, of unclear origin but possibly related to Old English wadan ‘to go’).

English: topographic name from Middle English wade ‘ford’ (Old English (ge)wæd ‘ford, especially a tidal crossing’) for someone who lived by a ford, or a habitational name from a place so called, such as Wade Hall in North Cove (Suffolk).

MORE

Irish: shortened and altered from Mac Uaid, see McQuaid .
