Brief Life History of Charles Wesley

When Charles Wesley Thompson was born on 17 May 1875, in Harrison, Ohio, United States, his father, George W. Thompson, was 42 and his mother, Margaret Busby, was 41. He married Effie M Isenhood on 20 June 1900, in Harrison, Ohio, United States. They were the parents of at least 2 sons and 2 daughters. He lived in Cadiz, Harrison, Ohio, United States for about 20 years and Short Creek Township, Harrison, Ohio, United States in 1940. He died after 1942.

Name Meaning
Thompson
Charles
Wesley

English: patronymic from the Middle English personal name T(h)om(me) (see Thom ) + -son ‘son of Tom’. Thomson is usually the Scottish form, that with the intrusive -p- being English. Both forms are common in Ireland. The surname Thompson is also very common among African Americans.

MORE

Americanized form of Danish, Norwegian, and North German Thomsen and of its Swedish cognate Thomsson. Compare Thomson .

Dictionary of American Family Names © Patrick Hanks 2003, 2006.
