Brief Life History of Jack Roger

When Jack Roger Wade was born on 29 May 1940, in Akron, Summit, Ohio, United States, his father, Thomas Samuel Wade, was 40 and his mother, Frances Della Thompson, was 37. He married Antoinet Ainsworth on 15 December 1974, in Los Angeles, Los Angeles, California, United States. He lived in United States in 1949 and Coventry Township, Summit, Ohio, United States in 1950. He died on 21 November 2008, in California, United States, at the age of 68, and was buried in Oakdale Memorial Park, Glendora, Los Angeles, California, United States.

Name Meaning
Wade
Jack
Roger

English: from the Middle English personal name Wade (Old English Wada or, via Norman French, from ancient Germanic Wado, of unclear origin but possibly related to Old English wadan ‘to go’).

English: topographic name from Middle English wade ‘ford’ (Old English (ge)wæd ‘ford, especially a tidal crossing’) for someone who lived by a ford, or a habitational name from a place so called, such as Wade Hall in North Cove (Suffolk).

MORE

Irish: shortened and altered from Mac Uaid, see McQuaid .
