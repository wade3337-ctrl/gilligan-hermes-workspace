Brief Life History of Frances Della

When Frances Della Thompson was born on 16 June 1902, in Wellston, Jackson, Ohio, United States, her father, Charles Wesley Thompson, was 27 and her mother, Effie M Isenhood, was 18. She married Thomas Samuel Wade on 19 July 1921, in Jackson, Ohio, United States. They were the parents of at least 1 son and 2 daughters. She lived in United States in 1949 and Coventry Township, Summit, Ohio, United States in 1950. She died on 20 February 1984, in Ohio, United States, at the age of 81, and was buried in Greenlawn Memorial Park, Akron, Summit, Ohio, United States.

Name Meaning
Thompson
Frances
Della

English: patronymic from the Middle English personal name T(h)om(me) (see Thom ) + -son ‘son of Tom’. Thomson is usually the Scottish form, that with the intrusive -p- being English. Both forms are common in Ireland. The surname Thompson is also very common among African Americans.

MORE

Americanized form of Danish, Norwegian, and North German Thomsen and of its Swedish cognate Thomsson. Compare Thomson .
