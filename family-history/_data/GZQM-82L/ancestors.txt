Brief Life History of Mary

Mary Mathieu was born on 3 January 1866, in Sedan, Ardennes, France as the daughter of Harry Eischen and Anna Petne. She married Louis S. Eischen on 16 April 1888, in Cook, Illinois, United States. They were the parents of at least 4 daughters. She lived in Shields Township, Lake, Illinois, United States in 1910. She died on 11 September 1946, in Waukegan, Lake, Illinois, United States, at the age of 80, and was buried in Northshore Garden Of Memories, North Chicago, Lake, Illinois, United States.
