4. **1945 CA County Marriage** attached to *both* John Ray Hanley & Bertha Ballew despite their 1914

   sourced KS/MO marriage in this branch is the **Hanley–Ballew 1914 (Wyandotte Co. KANSAS)**. Do not
   conflate the two. Webb City remains *Unverified* for anyone here.
6. **Birthplaces are state-only** (b. "Missouri"/"Kansas") for Dorcie, Rhoda, John Ray, Bertha — county/town
   not yet pinned from an image. All still *Strongly supported*, not *Confirmed*.
7. Multiple **surname OCR variants** (Hanley↔Hauley, Ballew↔Bellew↔Balleau↔Belew, Gaunt↔Gauntt,
