# 11. Important Source-Quality Notes

This project has mixed evidence types.

## A. Highest confidence
- Facts directly confirmed by Jason's aunt or other family members
- Original certificates and census images
- Government vital-record indexes
- Contemporary obituaries
- Church records

## B. Medium confidence
- FamilySearch indexed records
- Ancestry indexes
- Find a Grave memorials when sourced
- User-created family trees that match independent records

## C. Low confidence / leads only
- AI-generated genealogy summaries
- Statements inferred from surname ethnicity
- Historical storytelling not tied to a direct record
- "He was the right age for the Korean War, therefore he may have served"
- Claims about occupations, migration motives, or cultural identity without documents

Do not convert a plausible narrative into a fact without evidence.

# 18. Research Instruction to the Next AI

Do not simply repeat online family trees.

Use this hierarchy:

1. Original image of record
2. Government or church index tied to an original record
3. Contemporary obituary/newspaper
4. High-quality genealogy database entry
5. User-created family tree
6. AI-generated summary

For every relationship, ask:

**"What record directly links this child to these parents?"**

For every migration or ethnicity claim, ask:

**"What record identifies birthplace, nationality, language, or parents' birthplace?"**

For Adeline Eischen in particular, do not infer French ancestry solely from the surname. The objective is to document the French connection through actual records.
