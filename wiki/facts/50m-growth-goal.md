# 🎯 North Star: $50M gross sales in 5 years

**The goal (Skipper, 2026-07-16):** grow Great Scott from ~$25M → **$50M gross sales within 5 years** (~2× / ≈15%/yr CAGR).

**The strategy — win in 3 geographies:**
- **Inland Empire** — new-market PUSH (Riverside + San Bernardino counties). Ties to [[inland-empire-expansion]].
- **Los Angeles County** — new-market PUSH.
- **Orange County** — GROW THE BACKYARD. Skipper's read: *"we miss a lot of opportunity right in our own backyard due to weak business development practices."* Deepen penetration where we're already established.

**The lever = [[aspen-retention-agent|Aspen]] as the BD engine.** Aspen was created to strengthen BD substantially. First BD build: a **relationship graph** — Contact ↔ Property ↔ management-company (inferred from manager email domain) + $-value + geography + GSTS penetration + Scott's warm-contact overlay ([[apple-contacts-reconciler]]) — so Aspen can **rank the highest-value targets in the target areas** and explain why. "Leverage everything we have to drive this."

**How to think about it:** the graph surfaces two plays — (1) **deepen OC** (management companies where we service SOME of their portfolio, not all), and (2) **win IE/LA portfolios** (big management-company footprints we're under-penetrated in, where a Scott relationship gives a warm entry).
