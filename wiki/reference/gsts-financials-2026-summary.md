## Parsing / provenance notes
- Deck layouts are **not** uniform: only the January deck matches the "5-sheet" template (Budget / Break Even / Seasonality / history / month). Feb & Mar decks are wide 2023–2026 monthly time-series (`Sheet1`); Apr & May decks add clean 2026-only IS sheets. The **May deck's `IS 01.01.26- 05.31.26`** is the single most consistent 2026-actuals source and is used for all five months here.
- January actuals differ slightly between the standalone `Jan 2026` sheet in the Jan deck (COGS $1,269,949, GP $524,882) and the restated consolidated May-deck figures (COGS $1,258,644, GP $536,187) — the restated set is used here for cross-month consistency.
- Budget = **seasonalized** monthly allocation (not flat 1/12).
