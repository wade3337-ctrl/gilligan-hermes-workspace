# GSTC Employee Handbook (2026 draft) — legal-compliance flags

**Not legal advice — an internal, AI-assisted flag list for employment counsel (Roxanne).** Reviewed the extracted
[[gsts-employee-handbook-2026]] draft against California + federal law (Orange County = strictest wage-and-hour venue).
**Overall the handbook is reasonably well-drafted** — at-will language, meal/rest premium pay, 72-hour final pay,
Labor Code 2802 expense reimbursement, and the overtime rate tables are all California-compliant. Flags below.

## 🔴 High priority
1. **Field "Holiday & Vacation Pay Allowance" → overtime regular-rate exposure.** Field staff earn a per-hour allowance
   paid every check (§ *Holiday & Vacation Pay Allowance – Nonexempt Field*). If it's NOT included in the **regular rate**
   for OT (Labor Code 510 / Alvarado v. Dart), every OT hour for ~100 field employees is underpaid → PAGA/class risk.
   Also confirm wage-statement itemization (Labor Code 226).
2. **Discipline based on arrests/charges (Labor Code 432.7).** Two clauses act on an arrest/charge, not a conviction:
   *Criminal Activity | Arrests* ("involvement in criminal activity… on or off Company property") and the *Drug/Alcohol*
   self-report of being "**charged with**" an offense → discipline. CA bars adverse action on non-conviction arrests;
   Fair Chance Act (Gov. Code 12952) limits pre-hire conviction use. Narrow to job-related **convictions**.
3. **Cannabis testing vs AB 2188 (2024) + internal contradiction.** Drug policy bans "a **measurable quantity**… in the
   blood or **urine**" — urine detects non-psychoactive metabolites (past use), which AB 2188 protects; also contradicts
   the handbook's own off-duty-cannabis protection. Move to an impairment-based standard.

## 🟡 Medium
4. **Forcing paid sick leave onto unauthorized absences** — clause applies "unused vacation, **sick time**… to unauthorized
   absences." CA paid sick leave is employee-directed (Labor Code 246); employer generally can't compel its use.
5. **No-solicitation inconsistency (NLRA).** Misconduct list says "during **working hours**"; the standalone No-Solicitation
   policy correctly says "**working time**." "Working hours" is overbroad (sweeps in breaks). Align.
6. **Monitoring "telephone conversations" without consent** — IT policy claims right to intercept calls "without prior
   consent." CA is two-party consent (Penal Code 632). Carve out calls or add consent.
7. **Forcing vacation use during protected leaves** — clause may "require you to use any unused vacation during… family
   medical leave." CA limits this (esp. PDL / paid-leave interactions). "Where permissible" hedge — have counsel confirm.

## 🟢 Verify / lower
- **Paid sick leave amount** — confirm accrual meets CA's 2024 increase to **40 hours / 5 days** minimum.
- **Mandatory new leaves** — confirm **bereavement (AB 1949)** and **reproductive-loss (SB 848)** leaves are included (5+ employees).
- **NLRA savings clause** — good one exists on social media; extend the "nothing here restricts discussing wages/working
  conditions" language to the confidentiality/trade-secret rules.

**Caveat:** based on the extracted draft; an item may be addressed elsewhere — counsel should review the complete file.
