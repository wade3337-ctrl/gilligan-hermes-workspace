# $24M 2026 Operating Plan — Sales + Field decks + Bridge

**One-liner:** Two executive decks (built by the crew/Hermes) laying out how GSTS hits its **$24M produced-revenue goal for 2026**, plus a reconciliation **bridge slide** Gilligan built to tie them together. Crew-reviewed (Hermes adversarial + Judge acceptance). Numbers sourced from Nate's report / TRIM IT PLAY through **Jul 13, 2026**.
**Status:** 🟡 **Decks drafted + crew-reviewed 2026-07-14. Bridge slide built + delivered. Top-priority edits owed before the Skipper presents (see "Edits owed").**
**Relationship:** this is the *strategy/plan*; **[[revenue-goal-close]]** (RGC dashboard) is the *live instrument* that measures the same goal. Same $24M, DB-driven and adjustable.

## The two decks
- **Sales Operating Plan** (6 slides) — the *sell* side. Gap = **$4.06M** net-new ("protected additional-sales target") on top of **$19.94M** completed+sold/scheduled coverage. Close by **Oct 15**, produce by **Dec 31**. Required protected pace **$306K/wk**; market split **HOA $3.21M (79%) / Commercial $852K (21%)**; conversion **$5.54M proposals → $4.06M sold** (rep-rate plan). DRAFT rep allocations (Garrett $1.56M/66%, Rebekah $1.04M/82%, Scott $760K/75%, Ethan $699K/78%). Pipeline: 204 "running dry" accounts (24 unassigned). Weekly Mon–Fri operating system + five Friday numbers + trigger rules. Count-once discipline.
- **Field Production Strategy** (4 slides) — the *produce* side. Produced through Jul 13 = **$12.22M**; still to produce **$11.78M** over **118 workdays** = **$99.9K/workday**. Core equation **769 TotalHours × $130/hr ≥ $99.9K/day**. Scenarios Recovery(+8)/**CORE(+5)**/High-perf(+2) off June ~80 workers. June proof: $2.26M, $134.82/hr, 16,752 hrs, 80.18 workers, 9.07 hrs/worker. Recommendation: **operate the Core Plan (hire 5, hold $130+/hr)**.

## The bridge (Gilligan's add — the missing artifact)
The two decks measure **different gaps** and looked contradictory ($4.06M vs $11.78M). They reconcile to the dollar:
> **$12.22M produced + $7.72M sold-awaiting-production + $4.06M net-new = $24.00M.**
Field plan produces the $11.78M ($7.72M booked + $4.06M new); Sales plan sells the $4.06M. Both built off the same source (Nate's report: $19.94M grand total, ~$12.22M completed, ~$8.1M sold). **Caveat baked into the slide:** the $7.72M internal figure must be reconciled to Nate's ~$8.1M sold subtotal (~$380K gap) and segmented PO-firm vs PO-gated before quotas issue.

## Crew review verdict (2026-07-14, Hermes + Judge, independent)
**Headline flip:** the binding constraint on $24M is **production, not sales** — the sales gap is small and within capacity; the field plan is the heavy lift and is modeled on *peak-summer* numbers.
- 🔴 **Field likely under-hired for Q4** — the +5 hire rests on 9.07 hrs/worker (a *June* number). Winter daylight compresses it; at ~8.0 hrs/worker you'd need ~+16, and the same 85 workers fall ~$1.37M short. *Fix: re-run scenarios on actual Oct–Dec 2025 hrs/worker.*
- 🔴 **Rate erosion, same window** — $130/hr is hard-coded flat; Q4's Sold-heavy mix pushes it down. A 10% rate miss ≈ $1.2M.
- 🔴 **~$8M "sold" book isn't all firm** — municipal is **PO-gated** ([[municipal-budgets-po-gated]]); a 10% slip makes the true sales gap ~$4.87M (+20%), same deadline. *Fix: segment PO-firm vs PO-gated.*
- 🔴 **Bridge doesn't close to the penny** — $7.72M vs Nate's ~$8.1M = $380K unreconciled (~9% of the sales ask).
- 🟠 Close rate aggressive (73% blended; at 60% you'd need $6.8–7.4M proposals); pipeline "running dry" and **not yet dollar-scored**; the "$335K vs $306K pace test" is a strawman by the deck's own caveat.
- **Judge:** Field deck = **GO** (clean ask, June actuals). Sales deck = **GO-WITH-EDITS** (ask not stated; pace slide confuses). ⚠️ **Do NOT put draft rep numbers on the shared screen** — deliver 1:1 offline. **Present order: Field → bridge → Sales.**
