# Q2 2026 — Operations Summary
**Jason Wade, COO · Board of Directors, quarterly meeting**

## The quarter
- **Revenue $5.90M — our best quarter since Q4 2024**, and the second consecutive quarter of growth.
- **Revenue had fallen four quarters straight through 2025. That has reversed** — Q1 +5.9%, Q2 +11.0%.
- Q2 was **+11.2% over the same quarter last year**; Q1 was −9.3%.
- **Crew productivity hit $136.69 per crew-hour against a $130 target** — the highest on record.
- **We produced 15.3% more than last year's Q2 on only 4.6% more crew hours.**

## The first half
- **H1 revenue $11.22M — 91.6% of plan.** The entire shortfall was created in Q1 (85.8%); Q2 came in at 97.5%.
- H1 is **flat year over year (+0.4%)**, but on **5.5% fewer crew hours**.
- **Productivity for the half averaged $130.69 against the $130 target** — on plan.

## What that tells us
- **The operating engine is working.** Efficiency, pricing discipline and crew execution are all on target.
- **This year's productivity gain came from utilization, not speed** — non-revenue hours (yard, training,
  meetings) fell **29.3%**, from 22.8% of the day to 17.0%.
- **We are constrained on volume of hours, not on how we use them.**

## The gap to $25.1M
- Second half requires **$2.31M per month**, July–December.
- **The current plan of $2.2M/month does not get there** — it was built assuming an H1 of $12.25M that
  did not happen. As written it lands at **~$24.3M, about $800K short.**
- **$20.7M of the goal is already covered.** The uncovered balance is **~$4.4M of genuinely new business.**
- Closing it with productivity alone would need **$173.81 per crew-hour** — a 34% jump. Not realistic.

## What it actually takes — people
- **~76 field staff work on a typical weekday, already averaging 8.9-hour days.**
- Field headcount is **down about 8 people year over year**; the remaining crew absorbed it with no drop
  in total hours. **There is no slack left to ask for.**
- The goal requires **~95 working per weekday — about 19 additional field staff.**
- Every point of non-revenue reduction is worth ~205 crew-hours a month, and every $5 of rate is worth
  about one crew's monthly output — both reduce the hiring number, neither replaces it.

## What I am asking the Board for
1. **A decision on field headcount, with a date** — roughly +19. Staff added in October cannot deliver a
   Q4 number.
2. **Endorse re-basing the H2 plan** to a monthly number that actually reaches $25.1M.
3. **Back the utilization discipline** — if 17% slips toward 23%, the goal moves out of reach regardless
   of hiring.
4. **Acknowledge the crews' result** — target productivity through a soft quarter, above target in the
   second, on fewer hours than last year.

## Watch items
- **Retention** — recruiting has to outrun attrition before it adds any net capacity.
- **Q4 seasonality** — historically our softest quarter ($5.02M in Q4 2025).
- **Pipeline** — ~$4.4M of the goal is not yet sold.
