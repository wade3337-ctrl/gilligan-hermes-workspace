## 1. What "the Inland Empire" is (and our box)
- **Formal:** the Census **Riverside–San Bernardino–Ontario metro** = Riverside + San Bernardino counties, ~27,000 sq mi, ~4.6M people, east of LA/OC. ✅
- **No universally agreed boundary** ✅ — a recognized *narrow* definition (urbanized NW Riverside + SW San Bernardino) validates your western focus.
- **Our box:** Pomona/Chino Hills fringe → east to ~**San Bernardino city** → south down **I-15/I-215 to Temecula**. Excludes east of San Bernardino + all Coachella/Palm Springs.
- **⚠️ Caveat:** purists treat the **Temecula Valley** as its own region, not "IE." Defensible extension; doesn't affect the tree work.
