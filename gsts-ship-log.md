
### 2026-07-27 — iPad landing-page menu bug root-caused: a trapped z-index, not a touch problem [PLAY ✅ deployed · ✅ SKIPPER-CONFIRMED on IPAD **and DESKTOP** 2026-07-27]
- **Symptom (Skipper):** on iPad, opening a left-menu item makes the flyout render **underneath the Daily Close notepad**. Desktop is fine.
- **Structure:** the notepad is **two iframes deep** — shell `Profile$Main.HiRes.cfm` → `#CenterContentDiv` → `iframe ProfileBaseFrame` → `Overview/Quick$Base$WithList.cfm` → **`iframe DailyCloseFrame`** (`Profile.My.DailyCloseActions.Content.cfm`).
- **ROOT CAUSE — two parts:**
  1. **The submenu's own z-index is inert.** Spry sets the flyout to `z-index:1020`, but its container `#ActionMenuDiv` has `position:absolute` **+ `z-index:7`**, which **creates a stacking context**. The 1020 is trapped inside and cannot compete with anything outside the menu. The number that actually competes is **7**.
  2. **Nothing explicitly orders the menu above the content column.** `#CenterContentDiv` was `position:relative` with **NO z-index (auto)**. The menu won only via the implicit "positive beats auto" rule — correct per spec, honoured by Chrome/desktop Safari (**which is exactly why desktop works**), but **iOS Safari promotes iframes to their own compositing layers and does not honour implicit ordering across them.** Compounded here: nested iframes carrying fixed pixel heights (`750px`, `1735`), another long-standing iOS sore point.
  - Checked and **ruled out** the other iOS culprits: no `transform` / `opacity` / `filter` / `will-change` on `body`, `.gsts-shell`, `#LeftNavWrap`, `#ActionMenuDiv` or `#CenterContentDiv`.
- ⚠️ **I fingered the WRONG element first.** I blamed `#apDiv5` (`position:fixed`, bottom-left, `z-index:20`), built a headless-Chrome repro and *proved* the flyout loses to it — then checked what it renders on prod: **"Availability"**, the vestigial widget from 7/23, not the notepad. **The repro was right about a real defect, wrong about this one.**
- 🐛 **BONUS — a second, genuine collision found by that wrong turn:** the menu also loses to `#apDiv5`, and **on desktop too** (menu item ~24 of 46 at 1080px tall; ~item 11 on iPad). Proven via `elementFromPoint` at three viewports. The z-index bump fixes this as well; removing the widget (the 7/23 QoL fix) also kills it independently. ✅ **Skipper confirmed desktop working 18:39** — so the `apDiv5` collision was real and is now closed by the same two-line change. The wrong turn earned its keep.
- **FIX (CSS only, no logic/markup):** `#ActionMenuDiv` **z-index 7 → 100** · `#CenterContentDiv` **gains explicit `z-index: 0`**. Make the ordering explicit on both sides instead of relying on auto. Both rules carry a comment explaining the stacking-context trap and "do not lower below 21".
