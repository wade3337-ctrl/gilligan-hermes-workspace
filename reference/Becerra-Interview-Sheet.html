Fernando Becerra — Sr. Urban Forest Manager, ISA Certified Arborist

Andre = multidisciplinary landscape/arbor competitor active in Orange County (BOMA-OC vendor). He runs their arbor side.

1 · Verify Credentials (do this first — free)

  • Get his ISA cert number and verify it's real & current at treesaregood.org/findanarborist/verify
  • Ask what else he carries — these raise his value & what GSTS can bid:
  • TRAQ (Tree Risk Assessment Qualification) — key for liability & municipal work
  • ISA Municipal Specialist or Board Certified Master Arborist (BCMA) — top-tier
  • CA Pesticide License (QAL / QAC) — unlocks spray / plant-health-care bids

2 · Compensation

Market band — Sr. arborist / urban-forest mgr, ISA-certified, 14 yrs, SoCal (private sector):
~ $95K – $130K base
+ truck / benefits. Push higher only for advanced creds or a book of business. He's leaving a "Sr. Manager" title — the offer should feel like a step up.
⚖️ California law (Labor Code 432.3):
• You cannot ask his salary history ("what do you make now?").
• You can ask his expectations — "What are you looking for?"
• If he asks, you must share the role's pay scale.
His current pay is NOT public record (private employer — nothing to look up).

3 · Interview Questions

  • What are you managing now — crew size, account types, annual $ book?
  • Have you been the arborist-of-record on municipal contracts? Which cities?
  • Why are you leaving Andre? (motivation + any baggage)
  • What's your comp expectation? (per §2)
  • Walk me through a tough tree-risk / removal call you made and why.
  • How do you think about production vs. quality (our TPH world)?

4 · Diligence Before an Offer

  • Check restrictive covenants with Andre. CA voids non-competes, but a client / employee non-solicit is enforceable — matters if he brings accounts or crew.
  • Get references from supervisors, not just peers.
  • Confirm cert + any advanced quals in writing.

5 · Why He Fits GSTS (strategic)

  • Most municipal tree contracts require an ISA-certified arborist on staff to bid — he strengthens the muni pipeline directly.
  • Coming from a competitor in your OC backyard = market knowledge + bid experience + possible relationships.
  • One hire = added capability and a credentialed lead pulled from a rival.
