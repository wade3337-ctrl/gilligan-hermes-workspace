Arbor AI — The Edge (the competitive weapon)

AI agents that work on top of your data. This is where you actually beat competitors — not in the ERP plumbing everyone has.

  • Anomaly Monitor — LIVE — daily TPH / overtime / revenue / contract intelligence by email.
  • Salesperson Job Emails + Manager Rollup — PILOTING.
  • Municipal Tree Bid Manager — NEXT — the flagship: win-rate, pricing, the whole bid pipeline.
  • More agents over time — intake → sales → ops → billing, human-in-the-loop on money.

The Rails (what connects them)

Clean data + secure access endpoints. The AI can only be as good as the data it can reach — so making V1's data trustworthy and accessible is building Arbor's foundation.

V1.5 — The Foundation (TRIM IT, improved in place)

V1 works and runs the business today. We make it secure, friendlier, and AI-accessible — three buckets of work:

🔧 Fix what's broken

Security holes (SQL injection, the sa login, plaintext passwords), broken screens, bad data. → safe & trustworthy.

✨ Improve what's clunky

Friendlier, faster screens; less manual entry (Sales Pipeline, dashboards, Price Buddy). → pleasant & efficient.

🔄 Change the process

Streamline workflows. The biggest efficiency wins often come from how people work, not just code.
🔑 Key insight — same work, double duty. Cleaning and opening up V1 isn't separate from building Arbor — it is Arbor's foundation. Every fix gives two payoffs: V1 is better today, and another rail is laid for the AI tomorrow. One effort, two outputs — not two budgets.

Posture

  • Vendor (Data Processing LLC) → legacy maintenance only — keep V1 alive + the security fixes; not "build our future."
  • Watch-item: in-house AI = (you + Gilligan) today — powerful but single-threaded. Fine to start; plan to add capability as we scale so the edge doesn't ride on one person's bandwidth.
❌ What we're deliberately NOT doing: the $600–800K, 18-month "V2" rebuild. It's the same ColdFusion/SQL tech, and even done perfectly it only buys parity — it wins nothing competitively.
