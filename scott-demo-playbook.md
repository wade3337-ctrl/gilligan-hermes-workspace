## The arc — 6 questions that build to a wow (in order)

Each is tuned to land for Scott. Ask the ones we've **verified** Hermes nails. Cut any that stumble.

### 1. Warm open — "it knows us" (softball, sets the tone)
> "How's the business doing right now — give me the quick read."
- **Why it lands:** instant, confident, company-specific. Establishes "this thing actually knows Great Scott."
- **Want to hear:** a crisp snapshot — recent sales, production trend, a note of judgment. Not a data dump.

### 2. A number he feels — production/TPH
> "What's our throughput per hour running, and is that where it should be?"
- **TPH = Throughput Hourly rate** = dollars of production per crew hour (NOT "trees per hour"). Target 130.
- **Why:** TPH is the heartbeat number. Fast + correct + a read on vs-target = credibility.
- **Want to hear:** current TPH, the 130 target, whether we're above/below, plainly.

### 3. Depth — North vs South
> "Which yard is more productive right now, North or South?"
- **Why:** shows it understands operations, not just totals. Owners love apples-to-apples.
- **Want to hear:** North ~$127 TPH benchmark, the comparison, maybe why.

### 4. The salesman's heart — opportunity
> "Which good customers haven't we bid or touched in a while — where are we leaving money on the table?"
- **Why:** THIS is Scott's language. An AI that surfaces sales opportunities = love at first sight.
- **Want to hear:** named accounts / a "stale but valuable" list, a call-these-people nudge.

### 5. THE WOW — strategic judgment (our ace)
> "My ops guys want to cut the North crews from 45 hours to 40. What does that do to our revenue?"
- **Why:** this is the moment. We just did this analysis — Hermes should give the *nuanced COO answer*, not a number.
  When it reasons through OT saved vs. revenue-at-risk and flags the September pre-holiday surge, Scott sees a
  **thinking partner**, not a search box. Let the silence sit after this one.
- **Want to hear:** cutting to 40 = back to the 2026 budget; ~$0 summer revenue risk + ~$90K OT saved over the
  trough; BUT flex back up for the Sept pre-holiday surge. Seasonal lever, not a permanent cut.

### 6. Forward-looking — protect the revenue
> "Any city contracts coming up for renewal I should stay ahead of?"
- **Why:** ends on Hermes *protecting* money proactively. Ties to the city-budgets work.
- **Want to hear:** cities whose contracts lapse at 25/26 (Aliso, Cypress, Fountain Valley, Long Beach, Stanton)
  need a 26/27 renewal — get ahead of them.

## Run-the-room tips (for THIS audience)
- **Let Scott drive.** A salesman wants to poke it himself. Your job is to set up and get out of the way.
- **Sit so he sees the screen.** Shoulder to shoulder, not presenting AT him.
- **Fewer, verified questions > many risky ones.** 5–6 that hit hard. Don't improvise unknowns live.
- **After the wow (Q5), pause.** Let him react. Don't step on it.
- **If one stumbles,** smile, "it's still learning that one," move on. Never argue with the screen.
- **Leave-behind:** a one-pager so the vision survives after the laptop closes. (Ask Gilligan to build it.)
